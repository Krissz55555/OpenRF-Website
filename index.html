About OpenRF

More than firmware.

The OpenRF Platform aims to create an open, community-supported RF platform where hardware, firmware, documentation and the community form a single ecosystem.

The website is the central hub of this ecosystem — the place where everything from the first installation to troubleshooting, learning, collaboration and advanced development is available.

OpenRF Story

How a pool light became an open RF platform.

OpenRF Platform began with a simple real-world problem: controlling a 433 MHz RGB pool light without relying on its original remote. The solution evolved from a single-purpose controller into a reusable gateway with RAW learning, replay, MQTT, Home Assistant Discovery, OTA updates and a built-in WebUI.
